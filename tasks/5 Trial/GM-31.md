When publishing to the app stores is planned (the maintainer, 2026-11-23). Chat apps linkify only http and https, so `genbeya://` links aren't tappable there, on the phone or on desktop ([[GM-10]]). Until then, links open by sharing them into the app or pasting them into **Open a link**.

- [ ] A spec in [[Upstream]]: links on a domain Genbeya controls (`https://<domain>/open?remote=…&task=…`), desktop's **Copy Genbeya link** writing them and both clients accepting both forms
- [ ] A static page on the domain: in a browser it hands over to desktop Genbeya (`genbeya://`) or says how to get it
- [ ] Android App Links (`/.well-known/assetlinks.json`, with the release signing key's fingerprint) and iOS Universal Links (`apple-app-site-association`, with the Apple Team ID)
